The table rules

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards count as 10; aces count as 1 or 11.

Hit takes another card. Stand ends your hand. Double doubles your bet, deals one card, then stands.

Split separates a matching pair into two hands with equal bets. One split per round. Split aces receive one card each. A split-hand 21 pays 1:1.
